# Weinstein Stage Analysis — Methodology Summary

Condensed from "Secrets for Profiting in Bull and Bear Markets" by
Stan Weinstein. This is my own summary, not reproduced text from
the book. For the original source, see the book itself
(not included in this repo — see reference/ locally, gitignored).

## Four Stages
1. **Stage 1 (Accumulation):** Base-building after a decline. Tight
   consolidation, low volume, price bouncing off support.
2. **Stage 2 (Advance):** Markup phase. Breaks above resistance on
   volume, higher highs and higher lows.
3. **Stage 3 (Distribution):** Topping phase. Rallies show weakness,
   lower highs, volume divergence, sideways action at highs.
4. **Stage 4 (Decline):** Markdown phase. Breaks below support,
   lower lows and lower highs.

## 9-Condition Buying Checklist
Before flagging any entry, verify:
1. Proper stage setup — Stage 1 base transitioning to Stage 2, or
   early Stage 2 after confirmed breakout.
2. Price above its intermediate-term moving average.
3. Volume confirmation on breakout; contraction on pullbacks.
4. Relative strength (RS) improving or strong vs. the broad market.
5. Sector in a strong phase (Stage 1 or 2), not distribution/decline.
6. Broader market (Dow/S&P) in bull phase or at least not confirmed
   Stage 4.
7. Resistance breakout with authority, not a marginal move.
8. If buying a pullback: price holds above support, volume
   contracts during the pullback.
9. Risk/reward: stop-loss distance substantially smaller than
   measured-move target.

At least 8 of 9 must be met before an entry is actionable. Anything
less gets flagged as "monitoring," not a recommendation.
